Electric Race Car, Incubate Nepal

Incubate Nepal was an 8-week mentored research program, hosted by Harvard and MIT alumni. I was selected for this research from a competitive pool of 800+ applicants. Under the mentorship of MIT alumni Yaseem Rana, we built this remote control electric race car that was able to attain instantaneous 2g's vertical and 1.5g's horizontal acceleration. After thorough research on the aerodynamics and differential gears, we 3D modeled this race car. I assembled the electronics, fabricated the suspension mechanism and trapezoid suspension for the car. We 3D printed all the parts and completed the project with a successful test run.
